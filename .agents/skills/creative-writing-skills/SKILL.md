# Creative Writing Skills (Workspace Copy)

Cloned from `https://github.com/haowjy/creative-writing-skills.git`.

When crafting or refining video scripts or script-writing prompts, read and apply:
- `skills/writing-principles/SKILL.md` & `skills/writing-principles/resources/failure-modes.md` — trust the reader (never over-explain/moralize), economy (every line does double duty), protect curiosity/prediction reward, avoid AI punctuation tells (em-dashes).
- `skills/creative-writing-craft/SKILL.md` & `skills/creative-writing-craft/resources/` (`prose-writing.md`, `scene-construction.md`, `genre/thriller.md`, `genre/horror.md`, `genre/mystery.md`) — scene pacing, tension, transitions.
- `plugin/claude/skills/llm-writing/SKILL.md` & `resources/` (`word-choice.md`, `cold-read.md`) — eliminating LLM cliches and flat phrasing.
