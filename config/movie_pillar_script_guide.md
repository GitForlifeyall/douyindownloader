## The 4 Pillars

### Pillar 1: Speed to Value (`Hook -> Body 1 -> Re-Hook -> Body 2 -> Outro`)
- **0–3s Hook (Block 1):** Jump straight into concrete physical action + anomaly/conflict on frame 1.
  - *Action + Mirror Anomaly:* `"The woman touched her head. The monster in Korea copied her move."`
  - *Instant Trap:* `"The moment the girl lay in the bathtub, the door twisted shut."`
  - *High-Stakes Rule:* `"There was one deadly rule on this island..."`
  - *Clever/Extreme Setup:* `"He disguised a live bomb as chewing gum."`
- **Re-Hooks Every ~20–25s:** Reset viewer curiosity whenever tension settles (`"But the next second..."`, `"Suddenly..."`, `"Just then..."`, `"Little did he know..."`).
- **Crisp Outro:** Deliver a decisive payoff/twist resolution with zero rambling.

### Pillar 2: Story Flow (Single Hypnotic Through-Line)
- Connect every beat with strict cause-and-effect (`this happened -> SO he did X -> BUT Y blocked him`).
- Delete tangents / irrelevant backstory ("barrels on the sidewalk") that break viewer immersion.
- Stay 100% synced to what is on screen in that exact timestamp window.

### Pillar 3: Atomic Messaging (6th-Grade Comprehension)
- **Short Staccato Sentences:** 8–12 words average; no run-on clauses.
- **Active Voice Only:** `"He grabbed the stamp."` (not `"The stamp was grabbed by him."`).
- **Simple Everyday Vocabulary:** 6th-grade reading level; never use bookish words.
- **Clean Visual Labels:** Use clear character roles (`the man`, `the officer`, `the sniper`, `the boy`) and name key rules/objects cleanly.

### Pillar 4: Line-by-Line Self-Audit
1. Does Block 1 establish WHO + ACTION + CONFLICT within 2–3 seconds?
2. Does every line advance the main plot logically without tangents?
3. Are sentences short, active-voice, and clear on first listen?
4. Do re-hooks trigger roughly every 20–25 seconds?

### Pillar 5: Anti-AI-Slop Craft (`creative-writing-skills`)
- **Show Behavior, Don't Label Emotions:** Show physical action (`"He bolted the door."`) instead of naming feelings (`"A deep dread washed over him."`).
- **Hold Tension:** Never resolve suspense early or add preachy moralizing endings.
- **Zero LLM Clichés & Punctuation Tells:** No em-dashes (`—`), no `"Moreover/Furthermore/Additionally"`, no `"It's not X, it's Y"`, and one unambiguous noun per pronoun.
